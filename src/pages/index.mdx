# dandelion.art

<HomePage>

I'm Dandelion Mané—a creative technologist, an avid partner dancer (especially
Brazilian Zouk, Kizomba, Blues, and Fusion), and a spiritually curious human.

I've had the privilege of collaborating with many interesting teams over the
years. Some highlights from my career include:

- At [Google Brain](https://research.google/), I created
  [TensorBoard](https://www.tensorflow.org/tensorboard) and contributed to research
  including [Concrete Problems in AI Safety](https://arxiv.org/abs/1606.06565).
- At [Protocol Labs](https://pl.xyz/), I founded and led
  [SourceCred](https://github.com/sourcecred/sourcecred), developing algorithms for
  attributing value in open-source communities.
- With [Tyler Hobbs](https://www.tylerxhobbs.com/), I co-created
  [QQL](https://qql.art/), a participatory generative art project.
- As a founding engineer at Duramata, I built personalized AI agents.

I love discovering unexpected connections. That impulse shows up in software
that feels engaging and relatable, in dropping deeply into embodied connection
through dance, and in philosophically rich one-on-one conversations.

Right now, I'm taking time to focus on community and nonprofit projects,
including [Reverie Ballroom](https://www.reverieballroom.com/) in Seattle and the
[Matsya Foundation](https://matsyafoundation.org/).
