# QQL

<div className="qql-intro">
  QQL is a collaborative generative art project I created with Tyler Hobbs. 
  Tyler and I were exploring the theme of community curation in generative art, so 
  we made an algorithm with near limitless possibility, and invited generative art
  collectors to decide which QQL "seeds" would be canonicalized as official QQLs. The 
  images below are a small selection of the over three hundred QQLs that were officially
  minted.
